How to Play

Welcome to the Indian History Quiz! Follow these simple steps to test your knowledge:

1. Start the Quiz

Click the Start Quiz button on the home page to begin the challenge.

2. Answer Questions

Read each question carefully and select one of the four options provided. Once you select an option, your answer is locked in.

3. Instant Feedback

After picking an answer, green highlights indicate the correct choice and red highlights show incorrect choices along with immediate feedback.

4. Track Your Progress

Use the tracker at the top of the quiz card to keep an eye on your current question number and running score.

5. Review Your Results

At the end of the quiz, hit Submit Quiz to view your final score percentage and a complete question-by-question review.
